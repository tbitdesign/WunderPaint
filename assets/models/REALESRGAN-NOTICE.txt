Real-ESRGAN general x4v3 (realesr-general-x4v3.onnx)
====================================================

The AI upscaler behind Upscale (2x and 4x). Bundled with the plugin, it never
downloads anything and runs in the browser on the plugin's own ONNX runtime.

Source weights: the official Real-ESRGAN release v0.2.5.0 by Xintao Wang,
  https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN/releases/download/v0.2.5.0/realesr-general-x4v3.pth
  SHA-256 of that file: 8dc7edb9ac80ccdc30c3a5dca6616509367f05fbc184ad95b731f05bece96292
Architecture: SRVGGNetCompact (num_feat 64, num_conv 32, upscale 4, PReLU),
  as in realesrgan/archs/srvgg_arch.py of the same repository.
Conversion: exported unchanged to ONNX (opset 17, free height and width)
  with tools/export-realesrgan.py in the plugin's source repository; the
  ONNX output matches PyTorch to within 4.3e-6 on a reference input.
SHA-256 of realesr-general-x4v3.onnx: f788518cc66e302bc9b6946016e989dd9374f1012d5b6c0c7e753904ef3f99e4

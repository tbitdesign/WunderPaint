YuNet face detector — NOTICE
============================

File: face_detection_yunet_2023mar.onnx

This machine-learning model is used by WunderPaint to find faces in
photos locally, in the browser: Blur Faces pixelates them, and Collage &
Photo Grid keeps them in frame when it crops a photo into its cell. Inference runs entirely on
the client via the bundled onnxruntime-web; no image data is transmitted
to any server, and the model is never downloaded from anywhere: it ships
in this folder.

Model
-----
YuNet, a light-weight face detector, as published in the OpenCV Zoo.
The file is byte-identical to the original
(SHA-256 8f2383e4dd3cfbb4553ea8718107fc0423210dc964f9f4280604804ed2552fa4).

Original work:
  "YuNet: A Tiny Millisecond-level Face Detector"
  Wei Wu, Hanyang Peng, Shiqi Yu. Machine Intelligence Research, 2023.
  Source: https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_detection_yunet
